Account Recovery & Password Reset

If you have forgotten your portal password or are locked out of your account, password resets are processed in person to protect student identity.

Where to proceed:
  • QCU MIS / IT Helpdesk — Administration Building, Ground Floor
  • University Registrar's Office — Window 4 (Student Records Assistance)
What to bring:
  • Valid Student ID (or Certificate of Registration / Assessment Slip).
  • Official @qcu.edu.ph student email details.

Office Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
